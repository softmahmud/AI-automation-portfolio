# Real Estate Lead Qualification & Response Automation

## Overview

An automated lead management system built with n8n that captures property enquiries, evaluates potential buyers, categorizes leads by priority, and sends email notifications to support faster follow-up.

## Business Problem

Real estate businesses can lose potential customers when enquiries are handled manually, responses are delayed, or promising leads are not prioritized effectively.

## Solution

I built an automated workflow that processes property enquiries, evaluates buyer information, classifies leads, stores submissions in Google Sheets, and sends email notifications.

## Workflow

1. Capture property enquiry details through an online form.
2. Process the submitted information.
3. Evaluate the lead using conditional logic.
4. Categorize the lead according to its qualification criteria.
5. Store the enquiry and its results in Google Sheets.
6. Send an email notification with the relevant lead information.

## Technologies

- n8n — workflow automation
- Google Sheets — lead data storage
- Gmail — email notifications
- Conditional logic — lead qualification and routing
- Web forms — data collection

## Key Features

- Automated lead processing
- Rule-based lead qualification
- Lead categorization and prioritization
- Centralized enquiry records
- Automated email notifications

## Results

The workflow was tested with sample property enquiries and successfully processed submissions, stored the information, and sent email notifications.
